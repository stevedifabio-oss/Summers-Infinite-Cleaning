### Who is Summers Infinite Cleaning?

Summers Infinite Cleaning, formerly Clean Wishes, is a locally owned cleaning company in the Phoenix metro, owned by Summer Harrington. Summer and the team clean homes, rentals and businesses from Buckeye in the west to Scottsdale and Paradise Valley in the east and Anthem in the north, with eco-friendly products and a satisfaction guarantee.

## In Summer's Own Words.

“I'm a mother of four, and I understand the stress of a dirty house. It's frustrating and aggravating. Coming home to a clean house is what everyone wants. It gives you peace and clarity.

A clean home allows you to do more: to focus on your children and your home, and to focus more on your business. A clean home allows you to make more money, because you know the deal with being uncomfortable.”

Summer Harrington, owner
