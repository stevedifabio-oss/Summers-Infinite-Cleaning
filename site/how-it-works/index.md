## Four Steps to a Clean House.

1. **Ask for a quote**: Use the form, or call or text (623) 300-8785, day or night. A reply comes by call or text, usually within the hour.
2. **Get your flat-rate price**: One price for the clean, not an hourly rate. If it is your first clean with Summers, it is 20% off.
3. **Pick your day**: Choose a day from Monday to Saturday. Summers gives you a 2-hour arrival window, and nobody needs to be home.
4. **The clean, then payment**: Summers brings the supplies and gets to work. You pay on the day of the clean, by cash, check or Zelle.

- **Are you insured?** Yes. Summers Infinite Cleaning is insured.
- **Who will clean my home?** Summer and the team. The owner, Summer Harrington, and the cleaners Summers employs.
- **How long have you been doing this?** More than a decade. Summers has been in business for more than a decade, locally owned and operated.

## Questions About Your Cleaning Day.

### Do I need to be home for the cleaning?

No. Nobody needs to be home while Summers cleans, so you can be at work or away for the day. An empty home, before a move or while you are away for the season, is fine too.

### Is it OK if my dog or cat is home during the clean?

Yes. Pets are fine as long as they are put away while Summers cleans. Mention them in your quote request.

### When will the cleaners arrive?

Within a 2-hour arrival window on your cleaning day. Summers sets the window with you by call or text.

### How long does a house cleaning take?

About 2 to 3 hours for a standard clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home. For a different size of home, or a deep or move-out clean, ask about timing when you get your quote.

### How do I cancel or move a cleaning?

Call or text (623) 300-8785 at least 24 hours before your clean to cancel, skip or move it. With less than 24 hours' notice there is a $50 fee.

### What days and times do you clean?

Summers cleans Monday to Saturday, 7am to 5pm, with no cleaning on Sundays. You can call or text any time, day or night.
