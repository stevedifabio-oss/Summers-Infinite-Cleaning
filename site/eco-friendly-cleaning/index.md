## What Eco-Friendly Means Here.

Four plain answers, so you know exactly what the word covers at Summers.

- **Mrs. Meyer's Clean Day**: The products. The brand Summers cleans with. Look up any of its products by name to read the label.
- **Fragrance-free**: On request. Ask for a fragrance-free clean in your quote request, by text or on the phone.
- **Brought by Summers**: Supplies. The products and everything else for the clean come with the cleaner.
- **No certification claimed**: Labels. Summers does not claim EPA Safer Choice, Green Seal or any other certification for its products.

## Questions About Eco-Friendly Cleaning.

### Can I ask for a fragrance-free clean?

Yes. Fragrance-free cleaning is available on request. Put it in the details box on the quote form, or say so when you call or text Summers.

### Are your products EPA Safer Choice or Green Seal certified?

Summers makes no certification claim for its products. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency says taking part in its Safer Choice program is voluntary, and a product that qualifies can carry the label, so the label on each product is the place to check.

### Do I have to ask for the eco-friendly products?

No. Mrs. Meyer's Clean Day products are what Summers brings to a clean as standard. The one thing to ask for is fragrance-free, if you want it.

### Does a fragrance-free clean cover less?

No. Only the products change. You still get every task on the Summers list, from inside the oven and fridge to the blinds, ceiling fans and hand-wiped baseboards.
