## Prices, Quotes and Paying.

### How much does house cleaning cost with Summers?

Summers Infinite Cleaning gives free quotes rather than posting prices. To get yours, send the quote form with the service, your city and the approximate size of the property, or call or text (623) 300-8785.

### Do you charge by the hour or a flat rate?

A flat rate. Summers Infinite Cleaning prices each clean at a flat rate, and your free quote tells you what that rate is.

### Is there a discount on my first clean?

Yes. Your first clean with Summers Infinite Cleaning is 20% off. Ask for a free quote by form, by phone or by text to get started.

### How can I pay for my cleaning?

Summers Infinite Cleaning takes cash, check or Zelle. Payment is due on the day of the clean.

### Is Summers Infinite Cleaning insured?

Yes. Summers Infinite Cleaning is insured. If you have a question about it, ask when you request your quote.
