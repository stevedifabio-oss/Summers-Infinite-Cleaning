## What Happens to Your Details.

### Who we are

Summers Infinite Cleaning is a locally owned cleaning company serving the Phoenix metro in Arizona. This policy explains what happens to the information you share through this website. If you have a question about it, call or text (623) 300-8785.

### What the quote form collects

The quote form asks for the cleaning service you want, one quick question about the job (such as the size of the home, how often you would like a clean or the kind of business space), your city, your full name and your phone number. You can also give your email address, the property address and any other details you choose to type.

When you send the form, it also sends the address and title of the page you sent it from, the first page of this site you opened during your visit, the date and time, and a label saying the request came from this website. Nothing is sent until you press the button to send it.

### How Summers uses it

Summers uses your details to reply to your request by call or text, to prepare your quote, and to arrange and carry out any cleaning you agree to. Summers replies using the phone number you gave.

### Where your request goes

When you press send, the form sends your request over an encrypted connection to this website's own server, which is hosted by HostGator. The server then emails your request to Summers, and Summers replies by call or text.

The server also keeps its own copy of every request that reaches it, including requests it filters out as spam, turns away, or holds back because too many came from one connection in a short time. That copy holds everything the form sent (listed above), your browser's user agent (a short line of text that names your browser and type of device), and a coded (hashed) form of your IP address, which the server uses to limit repeated requests. The coded form is not anonymous: anyone holding the server's secret key could match it back to your IP address. The copy does not hold your IP address itself.

### How long it is kept

The website's server deletes its copy of a request after about 90 days.

The email with your request stays in Summers' inbox until Summers deletes it.

Requests sent through the quote form on the earlier version of this website are kept in a backup of that website for 12 months.

### Cookies, analytics and your browser

The pages of this website set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts, pictures and scripts load from this website itself.

While you browse, your browser keeps a note in its session storage of the first page of this site you opened, which is sent along with a quote request. If you press a quote button that picks a service on another page, the browser also keeps the service you chose, only until the form opens with it. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab.

### Server logs

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, for running and protecting the site.

### Asking about your details

To ask Summers about the details you sent, call or text (623) 300-8785.

### Changes to this policy

If the way this website handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
