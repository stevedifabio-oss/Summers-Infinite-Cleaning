## What a Turnover Clean Covers.

- **Every turnover**: Kitchen and bathrooms; the oven, fridge, cabinets and drawers cleaned inside; baseboards done by hand; blinds and fans dusted.
- **Add when the unit needs it**: Spot-cleaning the walls, plus inside windows, the window and slider tracks, and the vents.
- **Not part of it**: The garage and patio, moving furniture, dishes and laundry.

### What is a rental turnover clean?

It is the clean between one tenant moving out and the next moving in. At Summers it is Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, done in the empty unit with no one needing to be there. Summers brings the supplies and quotes a flat rate for each unit.

4. **Pay on the day of the clean**: By cash, check or Zelle.

## The landlord's clock

14 days

is what Arizona law gives a landlord to send the departing tenant an itemized list of deductions, with any refund owed.

The days run once the tenancy has ended, the unit is back in your hands and the tenant has asked for the deposit. Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays do not count, and a tenant who disagrees has 60 days to dispute the list.

Source: Arizona Revised Statutes 33-1321(D), part of the Arizona Residential Landlord and Tenant Act. A summary, not legal advice.

## Questions From Landlords and Managers.

### Do you send invoices for turnover cleans?

Turnover cleans are paid the same way as every Summers clean: cash, check or Zelle, due on the day of the clean.

### What should a landlord's move-out checklist cover?

Start with what Arizona law expects of a departing tenant: a unit kept clean and safe as far as its condition allows, trash cleared out and plumbing fixtures clean (A.R.S. 33-1341). Then walk the rooms: inside the appliances, cabinets and drawers, bathrooms, baseboards, blinds, ceiling fans, and window and slider tracks. (A summary, not legal advice.)

### Can the tenant book the move-out clean instead?

Yes. Renters book Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning with Summers as well, so a tenant can arrange the clean before handing back the keys. Landlords and managers use the same quote form.

### Can I get quotes for several units in one request?

Yes. List each address with its beds and baths and its move-out date in the details box, and Summers will reply with a flat-rate quote for each one.
