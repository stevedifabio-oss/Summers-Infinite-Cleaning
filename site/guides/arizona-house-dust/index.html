Why Is My House
So Dusty in Arizona?

Monsoon thunderstorms can whip dust off open desert and fields into walls of dust miles long, and dusting in the wrong order only moves it around. Here is where it comes from, and the order that keeps it off what you just cleaned.

Where the dust comes from

50 mph+

winds that monsoon thunderstorms can send out, raising very dense blowing dust over open fields and desert, says the National Weather Service in Phoenix.

The biggest become walls of dust and debris that can run miles across and several thousand feet high. They arrive suddenly and usually pass in a few minutes, but the fine dust they carry settles on sills, blinds and fan blades.

Why is my house so dusty in Arizona?

Valley homes sit in desert country, where monsoon thunderstorms between June 15 and September 30 can send winds of 50 mph or more across open fields and desert and raise dust storms miles wide. That fine dust settles on sills, tracks, blinds and fans. Cleaning from the top down, and getting the dry dust off before you wipe, keeps it from spreading.

Sources: National Weather Service Phoenix, Monsoon Safety and Monsoon Awareness Week pages.

After a Dust Storm,
Clean From the Top Down.

Dust first, and start high: Virginia Cooperative Extension's cleaning guide brushes the dust down before anything gets washed, the ceiling first and then the walls from top to bottom. Washington's Department of Ecology adds: wipe with microfiber or damp cloths.

  • Ceiling fans and light fixtures

    First, with the fan switched off, so whatever falls lands on surfaces you have not cleaned yet. Wipe the blades with a damp or microfiber cloth. Ceiling fans are part of every Summers clean.

  • Vents

    Vacuum the dust off the covers while it is dry. Vents are an add-on to a Summers clean.

  • Blinds

    Dust them slat by slat before anything below them. Blinds are part of every Summers clean.

  • Sills, window and slider tracks

    Vacuum out the dry grit first, then wipe. Window and slider tracks are an add-on to a Summers clean.

  • Shelves and furniture

    Wipe with a microfiber or damp cloth rather than a dry duster.

  • Baseboards

    Near the end, once the dust from above has come down. Vacuum along them, then wipe them with a damp cloth. Every Summers clean wipes them by hand.

  • Floors, last

    Vacuum and mop once everything above is done. Washington's Department of Ecology suggests a vacuum with a HEPA filter to capture the small particles.

Questions About Dust
in Valley Homes.

Does the Phoenix area get dust storms?

+

Yes. The National Weather Service office in Phoenix describes a dust storm as a sudden, advancing wall of dust and debris that may be miles long and several thousand feet high, usually brought by monsoon thunderstorm winds, and usually over in a few minutes. On August 19, 2026, one crossed the Phoenix area with gusts mainly between 55 and 65 mph.

When is monsoon season in Phoenix?

+

June 15 to September 30. The National Weather Service office in Phoenix uses those dates as the official season, the time when monsoon hazards such as damaging winds, heavy rain, lightning and dust storms are most likely.

Why is my house dusty again right after I clean?

+

Often the dust was moved rather than picked up. Washington's Department of Ecology recommends wiping surfaces with microfiber or damp cloths to remove dust, and a vacuum with a HEPA filter to capture the small particles. Working from the ceiling down, the order Virginia Cooperative Extension's cleaning guide uses, means falling dust lands where you have not cleaned yet.

How do I get dust out of window tracks?

+

Vacuum the loose grit out first, then wipe the track with a damp cloth. It is the dust-first rule Virginia Cooperative Extension gives for walls and ceilings: get the dry dust off, with a vacuum's brush attachment if you like, before anything wet touches it. Summers cleans window and slider tracks as an add-on.

How do I clean a ceiling fan without spreading dust?

+

Switch it off and clean it first, before anything below it, so falling dust lands on surfaces you have not cleaned yet. Wipe the blades with a damp or microfiber cloth, which Washington's Department of Ecology recommends for removing dust. Ceiling fans are part of every Summers clean.

Can I book a one-time clean after a dust storm?

+

Yes. Ask for a deep clean, a one-time clean that goes further than a regular visit. You can book any time with no set lead time. Summers cleans Monday to Saturday, 7am to 5pm, and answers calls and texts at (623) 300-8785.
