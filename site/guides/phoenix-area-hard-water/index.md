## Hardness by City, From Each Utility Report.

System ranges or averages for the year shown, not a reading for one address. Where a city has more than one water provider, check the name on your bill. gpg is grains per gallon; on the U.S. Geological Survey scale, up to 60 mg/L is soft, 61 to 120 moderately hard, 121 to 180 hard and above 180 very hard.

### How hard is the tap water in Phoenix-area cities?

Hard to very hard in most of them. Utility reports put Phoenix at 10 to 17.6 grains per gallon, Glendale at 13 on average and Scottsdale at 13 to 17. El Mirage's wells average about 7, and some Peoria wells run as low as 2. The USGS calls anything above about 10.5 grains very hard.

## Getting the Spots Off, Surface by Surface.

Methods from K-State Research and Extension's guide Homemade Cleaners, with two cautions from the New Jersey Department of Health and the Natural Stone Institute.

- **Shower glass**: Wash it with equal parts white vinegar and warm water and dry it with a soft cloth. For stubborn hard-water spots, use the vinegar undiluted.
- **Chrome faucets**: Cover the white crust with paper towels soaked in vinegar, leave them about an hour, then wipe the chrome clean.
- **Showerheads**: Soak a plastic showerhead for about an hour in a pint of white vinegar mixed with a pint of hot water.
- **Toilet rings**: Flush to wet the bowl, spread a paste of borax and lemon juice over the ring, leave it about two hours and scrub. A lighter ring may need only baking soda and the brush.
- **Two cautions**: Never use bleach with vinegar, lemon juice or other acid cleaners, toilet bowl and rust removers included: together they release chlorine gas, the New Jersey Department of Health warns. And keep acids off calcareous stone such as marble, which the Natural Stone Institute says they can dull or etch.

## Questions About Hard Water in the Valley.

### Why is Arizona water so hard?

Because the water picks up minerals before it reaches the tap. The City of Phoenix's 2025 water report says calcium and magnesium leach into the water from the soils it touches on the way to the city's treatment plants, and most of that supply is Salt, Verde and Colorado River water.

### How do I find out which water company serves my home?

Check the name on your water bill. Several Valley cities are split between providers: Goodyear between the city and Liberty Utilities either side of I-10, Paradise Valley among four companies, and parts of Peoria, Surprise, Buckeye and Scottsdale are served by EPCOR or other companies. Each provider publishes its own yearly water quality report.

### What is the difference between grains per gallon and parts per million?

They measure the same minerals in different units. A U.S. Geological Survey paper converts parts per million to grains per gallon by dividing by about 17.1, and for water, parts per million and milligrams per liter are the same. So Phoenix's 172 to 302 ppm is 10 to 17.6 grains per gallon.

### Does my tap water's hardness change during the year?

It can. EPCOR's 2025 Agua Fria report says customers may notice seasonal changes in hardness because the supply blends surface water and groundwater, and the City of Peoria says its mix of well and treated river water changes through the year and from one neighborhood to the next.

### Does vinegar remove hard-water spots?

On glass and chrome, often yes: K-State Research and Extension recommends undiluted white vinegar for stubborn hard-water spots on glass and vinegar-soaked paper towels for lime crust on faucets. Keep it off marble and other calcareous stone (Natural Stone Institute), and never use it together with bleach (New Jersey Department of Health).
