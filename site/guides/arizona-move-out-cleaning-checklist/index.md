## Room by Room, What to Leave Clean.

Work through it on the last day, room by room, once the boxes are out. The deposit timeline comes after it.

### What should I clean before moving out of an Arizona rental?

Every room, from the top down, with the floors last: inside the oven, fridge, cabinets and drawers, the tub, shower, toilet and sinks, baseboards, blinds and ceiling fans, closets and shelves, and every bag of trash out. Arizona asks tenants to keep the home as clean as its condition permits, plumbing fixtures included, and to get rid of trash cleanly and safely (A.R.S. 33-1341). A summary, not legal advice.

- **Kitchen**: Inside the oven and the fridge, inside every cabinet and drawer, the counters, sink and stovetop. The oven, fridge, cabinets and drawers are included in a Summers move-out clean.
- **Bathrooms**: Tub, shower, toilet and sinks: the statute asks tenants to keep plumbing fixtures as clean as their condition permits. Then the mirrors, counters and floor.
- **Baseboards, blinds and ceiling fans**: Dust from the top down, then wipe the baseboards. All three are included in a Summers move-out clean, with the baseboards wiped by hand.
- **Window and slider tracks**: Vacuum out the grit, then wipe the track and the sill. Available as an add-on.
- **Walls**: Spot-clean marks and fingerprints around switches, door frames and the kitchen. Spot-cleaning walls is available as an add-on.
- **Closets and shelves**: Empty, then wipe the shelves and rods so nothing is left behind.
- **Trash**: Every bag out to the bin, and nothing left in the cabinets, closets or garage.
- **Floors, last**: Vacuum and mop each room once the boxes are out and everything above is done.

### When does an Arizona landlord have to account for my security deposit?

Within 14 days, not counting Saturdays, Sundays or legal holidays, after three things have happened: the tenancy has ended, you have handed back possession, and you have asked for the deposit. The landlord then owes you an itemized list of deductions and any amount due (A.R.S. 33-1321(D)). A summary, not legal advice.

Source: A.R.S. 33-1321, subsections C, D and E, as published by the Arizona State Legislature. A summary, not legal advice.

## Ask for Your Deposit Back in Writing.

The 14 days wait for your request, so make it the day you hand back the keys. A short, dated note with these four things covers it. Keep a copy.

- **The rental's address**: With the unit number, so the request matches your lease.
- **Your forwarding address**: Where the itemized list and any refund should be mailed.
- **A request for the itemized list**: Ask for the list of deductions and the amount due to you.
- **The date**: So you can see when the count could begin.

## Questions About Moving Out of an Arizona Rental.

### Can I be there for the move-out inspection?

Yes. At move-in, an Arizona landlord has to tell you in writing that you may be present at the move-out inspection, and has to tell you when it will happen if you ask (A.R.S. 33-1321(C)). Ask early, then book your clean for before that day. A summary, not legal advice.

### Does the 14-day deposit deadline in Arizona count weekends?

No. The statute says fourteen days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and other legal holidays, which works out to about 18 to 20 calendar days, longer when a holiday falls inside it. The count begins after the tenancy ends, possession is returned and you have asked for the deposit (A.R.S. 33-1321(D)). A summary, not legal advice.

### How much can an Arizona landlord take as a security deposit?

No more than one and a half months' rent in security of any kind, prepaid rent included, though a tenant may choose to pay more rent in advance (A.R.S. 33-1321(A)). The purpose of any nonrefundable fee has to be stated in writing (subsection B). A summary, not legal advice.

### How will I get my deposit refund?

By first-class mail to your last known address, along with the itemized list, unless you make other arrangements in writing (A.R.S. 33-1321(D)). That is why the deposit request should carry a forwarding address. A summary, not legal advice.

### What if I disagree with the itemized list?

Dispute it within 60 days after the list and any refund are mailed. After that, the amounts on the list are treated as valid and final and further claims are waived (A.R.S. 33-1321(D)). Keep your dated request and any move-out photos together. A summary, not legal advice.

### What happens if a landlord wrongly keeps my deposit?

If a landlord does not follow the itemizing rule, the tenant may recover the money and property due plus damages of twice the amount wrongfully withheld (A.R.S. 33-1321(E)). For your own case, talk to an attorney. A summary, not legal advice.
