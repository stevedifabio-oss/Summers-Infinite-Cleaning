## Questions About These Guides.

### Where do the facts in these guides come from?

From the bodies that publish them: the Arizona State Legislature's text of the state's landlord and tenant law, each city's or water company's yearly water quality report, the National Weather Service office in Phoenix, the U.S. Geological Survey, the CDC, OSHA, the Carpet and Rug Institute, the National Association of Realtors and university extension services. Each guide names its sources where it uses them.

### Is the move-out guide legal advice?

No. It puts Arizona's security deposit law (A.R.S. 33-1321) and the tenant's cleaning duty (A.R.S. 33-1341) into plain words, and every statute line is marked as a summary. For a dispute over your own deposit, talk to an attorney.

### Can Summers do the cleaning these guides describe?

Inside the home, most of it. Every Summers home clean includes the inside of the oven and fridge, cabinets and drawers, hand-wiped baseboards, blinds and ceiling fans, and window and slider tracks, vents and wall spot-cleaning can be added. Dishes, laundry, moving furniture, garages and patios are not part of a Summers clean.

### Is there a guide for my city?

The hard-water guide has a row for each of the 16 towns Summers serves, each from its own utility report, and every one of those towns has its own page with local notes. The other guides apply across the Phoenix area.
