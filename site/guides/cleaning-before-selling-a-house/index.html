One Clean for the Photos,
One for the Walkthrough.

Should I have my house professionally cleaned before I sell it?

Plan two cleans rather than one: a deep clean in the days before the listing photos, and a move-out clean after the movers leave, so the empty house is ready for the buyer's final walkthrough. What the buyer can expect at that walkthrough depends on your purchase contract, so ask your agent.

  1. 1

    Before the listing photos: a deep clean

    Book it after you declutter and before the photographer comes. A deep clean is a deeper, more detailed clean than a standard visit. Like every Summers home clean it covers the inside of the oven and fridge, cabinets and drawers, hand-wiped baseboards, blinds and ceiling fans, and it takes on the hard-water film on the shower glass, faucets and toilets.

  2. 2

    After the movers leave: a move-out clean

    Empty rooms show every scuff and dust line the furniture hid. A move-out clean gets the house ready for the buyer's final walkthrough, and nobody needs to be there while Summers works.

What Shows Up in
Valley Listing Photos.

Hard-water spots. Tap water across most of the Valley runs hard to very hard, and it leaves a white film on shower glass and chrome. See how hard the water is in your city, and how to get the spots off.

Dust on sills and in tracks. Monsoon season, which the National Weather Service dates June 15 to September 30, brings dust storms with it. Check the sills, blinds and window tracks before the photographer arrives.

Questions About Cleaning
Before You Sell.

Does a seller have to clean the house before closing?

+

It depends on your purchase contract, so check it and ask your agent what it says about the home's condition at the final walkthrough. If you want the house ready for the walkthrough either way, book a move-out clean for after the movers leave.

Do agents recommend cleaning before you list?

+

Yes. In the National Association of Realtors' 2025 Profile of Home Staging, 88% of agents recommended that sellers clean the entire home, second only to decluttering at 91%.

Should I declutter before or after the clean?

+

Before. Decluttering was the step agents recommended most in NAR's 2025 staging profile, and clearing counters, shelves and floors first lets the clean reach every surface the camera will see. Summers does not move furniture, so set each room the way it will be photographed first.

Can you clean a listed home when nobody is there?

+

Yes. Nobody needs to be home while Summers cleans, so a listed house can be cleaned while you are at work or after you have moved out. Put the address and the date you need in the quote request.
