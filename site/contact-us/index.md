## Questions About Your Request.

### What happens after I send the quote form?

Summers Infinite Cleaning usually replies within an hour by call or text, using the phone number you gave. Sending the form asks for a free quote. It does not book a cleaning by itself.

### Can I text Summers?

Yes. Text (623) 300-8785 with your question or a few details about the place you need cleaned, and Summers will text or call you back.

### Can I ask for a particular cleaning date?

Yes. Type the day you have in mind in the details box at the end of the quote form, or mention it when you call or text. No set notice is needed to book, and Summers will confirm whether your date is open.

### What does Summers do with the details I send?

Summers uses them to reply to your request by call or text and to arrange any cleaning you go ahead with. The privacy policy, linked at the foot of every page, explains what the form sends.
