## Your Questions, Answered.

### Can I book cleaning on a regular schedule?

Yes. Book a visit every week, every other week or once a month, and you will usually see the same cleaner each time. Your first recurring visit is a deep clean, and recurring visits cost less per visit than a one-time clean. Say how often you would like a visit when you ask for your quote.

### Do you clean before or after a move?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleaning is for homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers when a Phoenix-area home changes hands. Nobody needs to be home, so the clean can happen after the last box is gone.

### Do you clean offices and shops?

Yes. Summers cleans any kind of business, from offices to retail spaces. Choose Commercial Cleaning in the quote form and tell us about the space.

### How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on this page, or call or text (623) 300-8785. Quotes are free and flat-rate, and the reply usually comes within an hour.

### Can I call Summers at night or on a Sunday?

Yes. The phone at (623) 300-8785 is answered 24/7, day or night, and you can text the same number. The cleans themselves happen Monday to Saturday, 7am to 5pm.
