## Before the First Box Comes Through the Door.

The garage and patio are not part of a Summers clean, and neither is moving furniture, which is one more reason to clean before it arrives.

### What does a new construction move-in clean include?

It is Summers' Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, done in a home nobody has lived in yet: inside every cabinet and drawer, inside the fridge and oven, the kitchen and bathrooms, baseboards wiped by hand, and blinds and ceiling fans. The inside of the windows, the tracks and the vents can be added.

- **Cabinets and drawers, inside**: Clean shelves before the plates, pans and pantry go in.
- **The fridge and the oven**: Cleaned inside, so the first groceries go into a clean fridge.
- **Kitchen and bathrooms**: Counters, sinks, showers and toilets, cleaned before anyone uses them.
- **Baseboards, blinds and fans**: Baseboards wiped by hand, blinds and ceiling fans dusted, with nothing in the way.
- **Windows and tracks, if you want them**: Window glass on the inside, window and slider tracks, and vents: ask for them as add-ons.

## Questions About New-Home Cleaning.

### What still needs cleaning after the builder hands over?

The parts you are about to use: inside the cabinets and drawers, the fridge and oven, the kitchen and bathrooms, plus baseboards, blinds and ceiling fans. A new house was a work site until the day it was handed over, so it is worth cleaning before you unpack.

### Is a new-home clean different from a regular move-in clean?

No. It is Summers' Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, booked for a home nobody has lived in yet. Choose that service in the quote form and mention that the house is brand new.

### Should the clean happen before or after the movers come?

Before, if you can. With the rooms empty, the cleaner can reach every baseboard, shelf and closet, and moving furniture is not part of a Summers clean.

### Do you clean for builders or contractors?

The new-home clean is for the people moving in, once the builder has handed over a finished house. If you are a builder or contractor with a different job in mind, call (623) 300-8785 and describe it.
