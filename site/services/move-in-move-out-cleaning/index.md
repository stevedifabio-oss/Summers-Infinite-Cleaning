## Questions About Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning.

### Will a move-out clean get my deposit back?

No cleaning can promise that. Under Arizona law (A.R.S. 33-1321), a landlord may apply a deposit to rent owed, to charges set out in the signed lease or in the state's landlord and tenant law, and to damages from a tenant not keeping the home as A.R.S. 33-1341 requires. A move-out clean lets you hand the home back clean, and you can dispute the itemized list. The move-out checklist guide walks through the deposit timeline. (A summary, not legal advice.)

### Should I check my lease for a cleaning fee?

Yes. Arizona law says the purpose of any nonrefundable fee or deposit must be stated in writing, and any fee or deposit not marked nonrefundable is refundable (A.R.S. 33-1321). (A summary, not legal advice.)

### Can you clean after I have moved everything out?

Yes. Nobody needs to be home for a Summers clean, so the home can be empty: after the movers leave, or before your own boxes arrive.

### How far ahead should I book a move-out clean?

There is no minimum lead time. Ask as soon as you know your inspection or closing date, and put that date in the quote form.

### Can you clean on the day I move out?

Ask about a rush clean. Summers takes same-day, next-day and end-of-month move-outs, so call (623) 300-8785 to check your date.

### What if my landlord flags something after the clean?

Report it to Summers no later than 24 hours after the clean; it will be re-cleaned or refunded, case by case. That is one reason to book the clean close to your inspection date.
