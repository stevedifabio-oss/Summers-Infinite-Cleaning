## Questions About Choosing a Clean.

### Is a move-out clean the same as a deep clean?

Not quite. Both cover the full Summers task list, but a deep clean brings a lived-in home back to clean, hard-water spots included, while a move-in or move-out clean is timed to the keys and works in an empty home with nobody there.

### Which clean is right for a home I am selling?

Book a deep clean while you still live there, ahead of the listing photos. Once the movers leave, Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning readies the empty home for the buyer, and nobody needs to be there for it.

### Should I book a one-time clean or regular visits?

Book once when the home needs catching up or you are getting ready for guests: House Cleaning or Deep Cleaning. To keep it that way, choose Recurring House Cleaning: visits come weekly, every two weeks or monthly, the same cleaner usually returns, and the first visit is a deep clean.

### Do you clean apartments, condos and townhomes?

Yes. Summers Infinite Cleaning cleans apartments, condos and townhomes as well as houses, from a regular clean to the end of a lease. Give the unit size in the quote form.
