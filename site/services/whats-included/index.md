## On the List for Every Clean.

A house clean, a deep clean, every recurring visit and a move-in or move-out clean all include these, along with the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas.

### What does each Summers clean include?

Every clean from Summers Infinite Cleaning, whether it is a house clean, a deep clean, a recurring visit or a move-in or move-out clean, includes the inside of the oven and fridge, inside cabinets and drawers, baseboards wiped by hand, blinds, ceiling fans, and making beds or changing linens. A deep clean also cleans hard-water spots off shower glass, faucets and toilets. Window and slider tracks, inside windows, spot-cleaning walls and vents are add-ons. Dishes, laundry, moving furniture, garages and patios are not offered.

- **Inside the oven**: Part of a standard house clean, not saved for the deep clean.
- **Inside the fridge**: On recurring visits and move-out cleans as well.
- **Inside cabinets and drawers**: The insides, not only the doors and fronts.
- **Baseboards**: Wiped by hand.
- **Blinds**: No add-on needed: they are on the list.
- **Ceiling fans**: Up high, where dust settles between cleans.
- **Beds made or linens changed**: Part of finishing the bedrooms.

## Questions About What Is Included.

### Do you do dishes or laundry?

No. Dishes and laundry are not part of any Summers clean. Changing the linens is included, but washing the used ones is laundry, so that stays with you.

### Will you move furniture to clean behind it?

No. Moving furniture is not part of any Summers clean, so beds, sofas and other heavy pieces stay where they are and the clean works around them.

### Is the garage or patio included?

No. Summers does not clean garages or patios, in a house clean, a deep clean or a move-out clean.

### Do you wash walls or windows in a move-out clean?

Not as standard. Spot-cleaning walls and cleaning inside the windows are add-ons for a move-out clean, as they are for any clean, and so are the window and slider tracks. Ask for them in your quote request.
