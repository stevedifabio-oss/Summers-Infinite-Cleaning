## How Often Is Right for You?

There is no single answer. These are the things that make a home need a clean sooner.

- **Who lives there.** More people means more traffic through the kitchen and bathrooms.
- **Pets.** Fur and paw prints add up fast between cleans.
- **How you cook.** A kitchen in daily use needs attention more often than one used for coffee.
- **The season.** Summer monsoon storms can stir up dust across the Valley.

### How often should I schedule professional house cleaning?

It depends on the household. A home with kids, pets or daily cooking gets messy faster than a quiet home for one or two. ### Will I get the same cleaner on each recurring visit?

Usually, yes. If having the same cleaner matters to you, say so in your quote request.

### Is weekly or every-two-weeks cleaning better?

It depends on how fast your home gets messy. A busy home with kids, pets or daily cooking may need a weekly visit, while a quieter home can stay on track every two weeks or once a month. Summers offers all three; say which you are weighing in your quote request.

Each visit is a standard house cleaning of the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas.
