Beds are made or the linens changed, and Summers brings the supplies.

### Is a deep clean worth it if the house was empty all summer?

It is your call. A deep clean goes further than the standard house clean, hard-water spots on glass, faucets and toilets included, and it suits a house that has not had a thorough clean in months. If the house was cleaned well when you left, say so, and ask about a standard clean as well.
