## Hard water in Phoenix

172 to 302

parts per million of hardness in City of Phoenix tap water in 2025. The U.S. Geological Survey calls more than 180 very hard.

Hardness means minerals such as calcium and magnesium, which the water picks up from soil on its way to the treatment plants. As hard water dries it can leave spots and film behind, and some types of scale on fixtures such as faucets and showerheads.

## Questions About Deep Cleaning.

### Should my first visit be a deep clean?

If you are starting recurring cleaning, yes: Summers makes the first recurring visit a deep clean. For a one-time clean, start with a deep clean when the home has not had a thorough clean in a while. If it is already in good shape, the standard house cleaning may be all it needs.

### Do you remove hard-water spots from shower glass and faucets?

Yes. The deep clean from Summers Infinite Cleaning includes removing hard-water spots from shower glass, faucets and toilets. Say where the spots are in your quote request.

### Are hard-water spots harmful?

No. The U.S. Geological Survey describes the spots and film that hard water leaves behind as unsightly rather than dangerous.

### Why does hard water leave soap scum?

Soap reacts with the calcium in hard water and forms soap scum, the U.S. Geological Survey explains, so more soap or detergent is needed to get things clean. City of Phoenix tap water falls in the range the USGS calls hard to very hard.
