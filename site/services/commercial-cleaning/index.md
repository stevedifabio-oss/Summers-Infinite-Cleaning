## Questions About Commercial Cleaning.

### What other business spaces can Summers clean?

Any kind of business. Beyond offices and retail spaces, describe your space in the quote form, and Summers will reply with a flat-rate quote for it.

### What do you need to know to quote an office or shop?

The address and city, the kind of space and roughly how big it is, the date you would like to start, and anything about access or parking. Choose Commercial Cleaning in the form, so Summers knows the request is for a business.

### Do you use eco-friendly products in offices and shops?

Yes. Summers cleans workplaces with eco-friendly products, as it does homes. If your space needs a fragrance-free clean, ask for it in the quote form.

### What if something is missed at our business?

Call or text Summers within 24 hours of the visit, and Summers will re-clean or refund, case by case, just as for a home.
