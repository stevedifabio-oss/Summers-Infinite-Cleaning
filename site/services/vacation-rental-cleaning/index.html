What Gets Reset
Between Guests.

Laundry and dishes are not part of a Summers clean, so keep a clean set of linens ready for each bed.

What does a vacation rental clean from Summers include?

Every bedroom, bathroom and the kitchen, with beds made or linens changed, the fridge, oven, cabinets and drawers cleaned inside, the baseboards hand-wiped and the blinds and ceiling fans dusted. Summers brings the cleaning supplies. Washing laundry and dishes is not part of it.

  • Beds

    Made, or stripped and remade with the clean set you leave out.

  • Kitchen

    Inside the fridge, oven, cabinets and drawers, so the next guest opens a clean fridge.

  • Bathrooms

    Cleaned for the next arrival, from the sink to the shower.

  • Dust

    Baseboards wiped down by hand; blinds and fan blades dusted.

  • Add-ons

    Spot-cleaning the walls, inside windows, slider and window tracks, or vents, when a stay calls for them.

Questions
From Hosts.

Will you change the sheets between guests?

+

Yes. Making beds or changing linens is part of every Summers clean. Washing them is laundry, which Summers does not do, so leave a clean set out for each bed.

Which towns do you clean vacation rentals in?

+

The same towns as every other Summers clean: Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix and the other cities on the service areas page.

How do I send Summers the dates for each stay?

+

Text or call (623) 300-8785 with each check-out date as bookings come in, or use the quote form for the first one. The phone is answered 24/7, and Summers usually replies within an hour.

Can you deep clean the rental between busy seasons?

+

Yes. Deep Cleaning goes further than the regular clean and removes hard-water spots from shower glass, faucets and toilets. Pick Deep Cleaning in the quote form and give a date between bookings.
