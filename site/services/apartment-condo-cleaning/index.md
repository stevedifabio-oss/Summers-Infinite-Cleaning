## Living There, or Moving Out?

Two kinds of clean for apartments, condos and townhomes. Pick yours, and the quote form opens on it.

- ![A sunny window seat with a squeegee and a folded blue cloth resting on the sill](https://www.summersinfinitecleaning.com/assets/bank/window-detail.webp) **Living there**. A clean of the kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms and living space, once or on a schedule: weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly. Recurring visits cost less each time than a one-time clean.
- ![Moving boxes stacked by a sliding glass door in an empty room with a bare oak floor](https://www.summersinfinitecleaning.com/assets/scenes/moveout-empty.webp) **Moving out**. A move-out clean once the boxes are gone, so the unit goes back clean. Nobody needs to be home, and Summers takes rush move-outs: same day, next day or the end of the month.

### What does an apartment or condo clean include?

The same work as a Summers house clean, sized to the unit: the kitchen, with the oven, fridge, cabinets and drawers cleaned inside; the bathrooms; hand-wiped baseboards; dusted blinds and ceiling fans; and beds made or linens changed. Summers brings the supplies. Extras on request: the inside of the windows, the window and slider tracks, spot-cleaning walls and the vents.

## What to Tell Summers About Your Building.

Apartments and condos come with gates, stairs and parking rules. Put these in the details box of the quote form, and the cleaner knows before setting out.

- **The floor, and the elevator**: Which floor the unit is on, and whether there is a lift or only stairs.
- **The gate or the front desk**: A gate code, a call box, or a front desk that needs a name in advance.
- **Where to park**: Visitor spaces, street parking, or a pass the cleaner should leave on the dash.
- **Getting into the unit**: Nobody needs to be home. Say whether you will be there or how the cleaner gets in.
- **Your move-out date**: For a move-out clean, the day the keys go back, so the clean is booked ahead of it.

## Questions From Renters and Condo Owners.

### Do you clean studios and one-bedroom apartments?

Yes. A studio or a one-bedroom gets the same list as any Summers clean, from the inside of the oven and fridge to the baseboards. In the quote form, choose the closest size, 1 bed and 1 bath, and add that it is a studio.

### Is cleaning an apartment any different from cleaning a house?

The cleaning is the same: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living space, with the oven, fridge, cabinets, baseboards, blinds and fans. What changes is reaching the unit, which is why Summers asks about the floor, the gate and parking.

### Can Summers clean my apartment before the move-out inspection?

Yes. Book a move-out clean for after the movers leave and before the inspection. Arizona law lets you ask the landlord for the inspection date and be present for it (A.R.S. 33-1321). No clean can promise a deposit back. (A summary, not legal advice.)

### I just bought a condo. Can it be cleaned before I move in?

Yes. Choose Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning in the quote form and give the closing date and the day your furniture comes. Nobody needs to be there, so the clean can fit in between the two.
