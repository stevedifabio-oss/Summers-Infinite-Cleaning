## Questions About House Cleaning.

### Can I point out a room that needs extra care?

Yes. The quote form has a details box for exactly that. Name the room, and add anything else Summers should know before a house cleaning, like pets or where to park.

### Do I need to know my home's square footage?

No. Beds and baths are enough for the size box on the quote form, for example 3 beds, 2 baths. Add the square footage only if you have it handy.

### Do I need to supply anything for a house cleaning?

No. Summers brings the cleaning supplies and the equipment. If you would like a fragrance-free clean, ask for it in your quote request.

### Can I add extras like inside windows or vents?

Yes. Window and slider tracks, inside windows, spot-cleaning walls and vents can be added to any clean. Ask for them in your quote request.
