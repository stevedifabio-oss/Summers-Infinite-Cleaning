## Cleaning in Scottsdale.

Very hard water, and homes kept for part of the year.

![The corner of a black window frame against perfectly clear glass reflecting green leaves](https://www.summersinfinitecleaning.com/assets/bank/window.webp)

- **[Tap water](https://www.summersinfinitecleaning.com/guides/phoenix-area-hard-water/)**: 13 to 17 grains per gallon. Very hard on the US Geological Survey's scale, the sort that clouds glassware with spots and leaves a film of soap scum in showers. 71% is Colorado River water brought in by the Central Arizona Project canal, 13% comes from the Salt River Project and 16% from wells and recycled water. Sources: Scottsdale Water, 2026 Water Quality Report; USGS Water Science School.
- **Part-year homes**: About one home in eleven. The Census Bureau counts 12,314 Scottsdale homes held for seasonal, recreational or occasional use, and about a third of the city's homes are rented. Source: US Census Bureau, American Community Survey.

### Is Scottsdale's water harder in some neighborhoods?

A little. Scottsdale Water puts homes south of Indian School Road at 13 to 15 grains per gallon, and homes north of it, up past Chaparral Road, at 13 to 17. Parts of Scottsdale on EPCOR's Chaparral City system get water EPCOR measures at about 16.
