### Why do Anthem faucets and shower doors crust over so quickly?

Very hard water. EPCOR's 2025 report puts Anthem's water at about 18 grains per gallon, well past the 10.5 where its scale says very hard begins. The crust is mostly calcium carbonate, and a Summers deep clean takes those spots off the shower glass, the faucets and the toilets.

### Does Anthem's water change with the seasons?

It can. Anthem's supply is mostly Colorado River water, which arrives through the Central Arizona Project canal and is blended with groundwater, and EPCOR says that mix can shift the hardness and taste from season to season. EPCOR calls the difference normal and completely safe.
