### Can I get a move-in clean for a brand-new Buckeye home?

Yes. Summers cleans new homes after the builder hands them over, and nobody needs to be there, so the house can be ready the day you arrive. Mention that it is newly built when you ask for your quote.

### Buckeye runs on well water. Does that matter for the clean?

It can. The City of Buckeye says the calcium, sodium and magnesium in its groundwater are not pleasing in smell, taste or hardness, and the USGS ties hard water to soap scum and spotted glass. If they have built up on your shower glass or faucets, a deep clean is the one to book.

### Most Buckeye homes are fairly new. Do they need a deep clean?

Not always. More than half of Buckeye homes were built in 2010 or later, and one that has been kept up may only need a standard clean and regular visits. Ask for a deep clean when the glass or fixtures have spotted, or when you start recurring cleaning, whose first visit is a deep clean.
