## Hard Water Around the Valley.

### Which towns on this page have the hardest tap water?

Avondale, going by the averages in the water reports: its city water averaged 20.9 grains per gallon in 2022. Anthem's EPCOR water runs about 18, the same as the City of Goodyear's water south of I-10 averaged in its 2022 report, and Peoria's Pyramid Peak plant averages 17. All four are well past the 10.5 grains where the USGS scale starts calling water very hard, the kind behind spotted glasses and soap scum.

### Does any city near Phoenix have soft tap water?

Not really. Of the cities whose water reports give an average, El Mirage's is the lowest: about 7 grains per gallon from nine city wells, which the USGS scale still counts as moderately hard. Some Peoria city wells run 2 to 8 grains, and EPCOR's Sun City West range starts at 3.

## Monsoon Season Runs June 15 to September 30.

That is the National Weather Service's window for the Valley's summer storms, which can bring damaging winds, heavy rain and walls of dust miles wide. Once it ends, a deep clean is a fresh start after a summer of dust.
