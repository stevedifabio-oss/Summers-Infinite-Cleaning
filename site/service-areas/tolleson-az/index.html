Do you clean in Tolleson, AZ?

+

Yes. Tolleson homes, rentals and businesses can book any Summers Infinite Cleaning service, from a regular house clean to a deep clean, a move-out or office cleaning. Call or text (623) 300-8785 whenever it suits you, or send the form.

Does Tolleson's water leave spots on glass?

+

Often, yes. Tolleson's 2023 water report measured hardness at 95 to 230 parts per million, about 5.5 to 13.4 grains per gallon: moderately hard at the low end and very hard at the top, on the USGS scale. A deep clean is the one that removes them from shower glass and fixtures.

I rent in Tolleson. Does a move-out clean cover the oven and fridge?

+

Yes. Every Summers clean, move-outs included, covers the inside of the oven, the fridge, the cabinets and the drawers, plus blinds, ceiling fans and hand-wiped baseboards. Window and slider tracks, inside windows, wall spots and vents can be added.

Can Summers clean the offices at my Tolleson business?

+

Yes. Summers cleans any kind of business, and Tolleson, in the city's own words, is the West Valley's industrial employment center. Share the kind of space, its size and how often it needs cleaning, and you will get a flat-rate quote.

Cleaning in
Tolleson.

A farm town from 1912, now crossed by I‑10 and the railroad, with many renters and mostly Phoenix water.

  • Renters

    About 58% of homes are rented

    Against 43% in Phoenix, by the Census Bureau's estimate. Two‑ and three‑bedroom homes make up about three in four.

    Source: US Census Bureau, renter and bedroom counts, 2020 to 2024.

  • Tap water

    88% from Phoenix in 2023

    Tolleson buys most of its water from the City of Phoenix through two connections, and treats water from its own wells for the rest.

    Source: City of Tolleson 2023 water quality report.

  • Homes

    Median year built: 1996

    39% went up between 2000 and 2009, and about one in six in the 1950s, when Tolleson was known as the Vegetable Center of the World.

    Sources: American Community Survey; City of Tolleson history.
