Where Glendale's water comes from, and how long its houses have stood.

  • Tap water

    13 grains per gallon on average

    From 8.9 to 18 across the city's 2023 tests. 46% is Salt and Verde river water from the Salt River Project, 43% Colorado River water from the Central Arizona Project canal, and the other 11% reclaimed water and wells.

    Sources: City of Glendale water quality reports for 2023 and 2024.

  • Older homes

    Median year built: 1986

    Half of Glendale's homes were built in 1986 or earlier, 21% in the 1990s and fewer than one in ten since 2010. A home lived in that long is a good candidate for a deep clean before recurring visits start.

    Source: US Census Bureau, American Community Survey.

Is Glendale building many new homes?

Some. The Census Bureau's permit survey shows Glendale approving 476 new single-family homes in 2025 and no apartment buildings. Moving into one? Ask for a move-in clean before the furniture arrives.
